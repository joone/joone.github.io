South Korean president Roh Moo-hyun and North Korean leader Kim Jong-il held a historic summit meeting in Pyongyang, North Korea.

This is the second summit between the two Koreas.

During the summit, the two leaders held several talks about Korean peace, economic investment, and reunification of the two Koreas.

I hope that the two Koreas can achieve peace and prosperity on the Korean Peninsula.
