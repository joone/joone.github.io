The Korean national football team triumphed against Togo in their first match at the World Cup football competition in Germany. It was a valuable match because it was Korea's first-ever FIFA World Cup win outside of Asia.

Korea was unstable in the first half of the game and conceded the first goal to Togo. But we scored two goals in succession in the second half with a spirited fightback.

It was a very nice match. I look forward to winning the next match.
