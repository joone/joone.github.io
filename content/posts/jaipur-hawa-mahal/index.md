You may believe it or not, but I'm now in India. This is a business trip that includes a weekend, so I went to Jaipur over the weekend. Jaipur is one of the cities in India, and there are many beautiful palaces and castles to see. This is a picture of a palace. The palace was built for the women who lived there. Because they could not go outside the palace, they could watch the market from the windows.
