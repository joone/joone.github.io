I attended BlinkOn9 from April 18 to 19, 2018. Here is the official page: [bit.ly/blinkon9-info](http://bit.ly/blinkon9-info), the [presentations](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1WrbTcTLskQBX1r4QIoEFUnZY1yPWT7cIYn-kvWCbelc/edit?ts=5ad688a5#gid=0), and the [Lightning Talks](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1DovPnWWSXuG8PRyN-HT7R9gZ4PLihly5ZepqZJ3vmAc/edit#gid=0).

At this conference, I talked about [accelerating graphics performance with ozone-gbm on Intel-based Linux desktop systems](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1o-a-DV43SnPPeyQodeMdbIqA05bRTNpZ3uidP2CBYeo/edit#slide=id.g38a9ffee37_0_0): I explained what kinds of hardware acceleration have been applied to Intel-based Chromebooks, and how we can use them on a Linux desktop.
