date: 2006-02-08
description: ""
tags: "Apple, hardware, personal"

At last, I bought a video iPod — the fifth generation of the iPod family. It is
thinner and lighter than I expected. The iPod is very easy for me to use because
I already use iTunes on my Mac, and I have many MP3 and video files in my iTunes
library. The one catch is that I have to resize video files before moving them to
the iPod. Fortunately, iTunes can resize videos that have a large screen size so
they play well on the iPod. Converting the video size takes a long time, of
course. Anyway, it's great to enjoy videos on my new iPod while I'm on the move.
