The Mozilla project and Seneca College have worked together on Mozilla-related
research and development, and they offer several courses on Mozilla and open
source.

[The results are posted on the project wiki](http://zenit.senecac.on.ca/wiki/index.php/Main_Page),
where you can find various projects and technical reports created by students. In
particular, there is a course called "Real World Mozilla" that guides students
through developing on the Mozilla platform — from building to testing Firefox. It
is helpful for anyone interested in Mozilla development, because it is documented
well on the wiki.

In Korea, [Daum](http://info.daum.net/eng/main.jsp) and Jeju National University
created an open source course together. I think that if more organizations build
and share results from open source projects like this, it will help foster open
source culture and movements in Korea.
