Do you know about BarCamp?

BarCamp is an informal meeting that doesn't have a fixed subject. Every attendee can talk about anything without formality. Just talking is fine, and a presentation is no problem too. Each speaker has 20 minutes, including Q&A.

This was the first BarCamp held in Korea. Some companies supported it, including Daum, OnNet, and TatterTools.

I was delighted to have the opportunity to attend, because the number of attendees was limited to 50.

I gave a presentation about the story of programmers. This story is based on my blog.

This BarCamp was very useful, and I would like to attend the next one.
